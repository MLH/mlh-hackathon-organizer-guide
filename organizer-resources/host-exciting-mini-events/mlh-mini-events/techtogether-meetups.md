# TechTogether Meetups

Purpose: The meetups will provide underrepresented gender participants and their allies with opportunities to connect, share ideas, and discuss industry challenges at the hackathon. We aim to enhance the overall hackathon experience and promote an inclusive atmosphere.

**These are fully opt-in for organizers. If you no longer can host one, let the Coach attending know. The goal is not to be stressful on finding an timeslot.**

You should aim to find a space where the group can talk casually. A circle of seats, a lounge, or a nice outdoor space weather permitting. You should not have these meetups in a classroom space if possible, or you should try to move classroom setup to be more group focused instead of presentation focused. We have seen great success when it is host during mealtimes/right next to them!\
\
Some events partner with relevant local orgs (SWE, WiC, etc) to help lead this. We highly encourage this, our Coaches can give them our guided questions if they want but we want to encourage local connections!\
\
Some events also provide an alternative activity, this is also fine! We are super flexible with this offering. Examples are sometimes it turns into a more formal panel of underrepresented speakers answering questions, or on the opposite end turning more casual while making bracelets.&#x20;

***

Overall, the goal is to create a respectful, inclusive space where everyone—especially underrepresented voices—feels empowered to participate. Please ask your HCM for more details if you have any other questions!
