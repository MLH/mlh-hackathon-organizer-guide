# Lanyards for your event

Starting with the 2027 season, MLH Member Events can opt in for free lanyards from MLH. Each badge comes pre-clipped to a lanyard so your team can hand them out right away.

The quantity will be based on the attendee estimate in your event application, plus 100 extra to help cover organizers, volunteers, speakers, mentors, and other event staff. Lanyards are an optional benefit, so if your event already has its own or doesn’t need them, you can opt out.
